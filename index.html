FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about AYO — RUOK.

What is AYO — RUOK?

AYO — RUOK is a premium Discord security and moderation bot designed to protect servers from raids, spam, and malicious activities while providing powerful moderation tools.

How do I invite the bot to my server?

Simply click the "Invite Bot" button on our website, or visit the Discord bot marketplace to add AYO — RUOK to your server with the required permissions.

Is AYO — RUOK free to use?

AYO — RUOK offers both free and premium tiers. The free version includes essential security features, while premium unlocks advanced tools and priority support.

How does the verification system work?

Our verification system uses multiple layers of checks including CAPTCHA, member age verification, and behavior analysis to ensure only real users join your community.

Can I customize the moderation settings?

Yes! AYO — RUOK provides extensive customization options including custom punishment thresholds, whitelist/blacklist management, and role-based permissions.

What happens if the bot goes offline?

AYO — RUOK is designed with 99.9% uptime and automatic failover. In the rare event of an outage, the bot automatically reconnects and resumes all security protocols.
